# /1on1 — pre-1:1 context pull

Usage: `/1on1 <name>` — e.g. `/1on1 Marta`, `/1on1 Kuba`.

## What to produce

A pre-1:1 briefing in this exact order:

1. **Two-line summary** — what they have been working on the last 2 weeks, and how it tracks against `goals.yaml` priorities.
2. **What's on their plate (from GitHub)** — open items assigned to them on project #42, ranked by priority and age.
3. **Recent signals** — anything they have raised in Slack worth following up on (questions, blockers, ideas).
4. **From last 1:1** — open items I committed to, and open items they committed to.
5. **2-3 topics worth raising** — grounded in the above. Mark each as **Coaching** (they have a decision to make), **Mentoring** (they want to learn from my path), or **Performance** (calibration).
6. **What I should NOT raise** — anything already on track that does not need my time.

## What to do

- Resolve name to GitHub handle from `contacts/team.yaml`.
- Pull their open issues on project #42 and PRs from the last 14 days.
- Search Slack for their recent posts in #data and DMs to me.
- Pull the last 1:1 notes from Notion if present.

## Format rules

- Maximum 400 words.
- No psychoanalysis. Describe behaviour and work, not motivation.
- Frame each topic as Coaching, Mentoring, or Performance. Do not blur them.
- End with the one topic that matters most if we only have 15 minutes instead of 30.
